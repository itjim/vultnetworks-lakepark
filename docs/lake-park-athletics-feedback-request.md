# Lake Park Athletics — Independent Site Review Request

**Status:** Prepared draft only. Not sent. No school affiliation or endorsement has been requested or granted.

**Suggested recipient:** Lake Park High School Athletics Department / Athletic Director. Confirm the current recipient and permission to contact before sending.

## Proposed email

**Subject:** Request for feedback — independent Lake Park sports analytics demonstration

Hello,

I operate Vult Networks, LLC, and have been developing an independent, informational sports analytics demonstration centered on publicly available Lake Park High School athletic results. The site is not operated by or affiliated with Lake Park High School, District 108, or IHSA.

It organizes publicly reported game scores from IHSA and MaxPreps, calculates unofficial team records, and offers comparisons of opponents and conference-member matchups. The current pilot includes football (boys tackle and girls flag), along with navigation for other IHSA team sports. Where a feed is incomplete, the site reports its limitations instead of presenting missing games as losses or implying that sub-varsity teams have no games.

Before sharing it more broadly, would someone in the Athletics Department be willing to review the demonstration and offer feedback on accuracy and usefulness? I am especially interested in:

1. Whether sport, gender, team-level, and season labels accurately represent Lake Park programs.
2. Which source is best for JV, freshman, and any sophomore schedules and scores.
3. Whether published varsity game totals match the school’s records, including postseason events.
4. How conference-designated contests should be distinguished from other games against conference schools.
5. Which comparisons would genuinely help coaches, families, and the athletics department.
6. Whether any disclaimer, source attribution, or use of school references should be adjusted.

No private student information, roster exports, or account access is requested. The website is a technology demonstration, not an official school information source, and I will readily correct or remove inaccurate material.

Preview (for your review only): https://deploy-preview-1--vultnetworks-lakepark.netlify.app

Thank you for any guidance.

Vult Networks, LLC
