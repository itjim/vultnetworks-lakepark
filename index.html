About this site & data sources

Vult Networks Sports Analytics is an independently developed demonstration designed to organize and analyze publicly available Illinois high school athletics information. Game results, schedules, and school information may be sourced from the Illinois High School Association (IHSA) website and other public resources when data integration is enabled. Any standings, comparisons, statistical calculations, or playoff scenarios are independently generated by Vult Networks and are not official determinations.

This site is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or operated by IHSA, Lake Park High School, Lake Park Community High School District 108, or any school or conference. Source information may be incomplete, delayed, corrected, or unavailable. Verify schedules, scores, eligibility, and postseason decisions with IHSA or the relevant school. Current inputs are simulated examples, not actual team performance.
