About the data

Vult Networks Sports Analytics independently organizes public athletic results, including information obtained from the Illinois High School Association website or an undocumented API. Results may be missing, delayed, corrected or incompletely classified. Data provenance and fetch coverage are displayed; records, rankings, trends and comparisons are independently computed rather than official IHSA determinations.

Some sports, team divisions and sub-varsity levels are not separately identified in the source. Those selections display an explicit limitation instead of mixing results from different teams. We do not collect student accounts or individual player profiles.
